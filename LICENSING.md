## Default licence

Apache License 2.0 (SPDX: Apache-2.0) is the default licence for this project's original material and incoming contributions. The full text is in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Existing third-party material keeps its own terms; this policy does not override any other copyright holder's rights or existing notices.

## Additional permission from the project owner

The project owner additionally grants permission under the [Zero-Clause BSD licence](LICENSES/0BSD.txt) for code to the extent the owner holds the copyright and has authority to license it. For those portions, recipients may choose either Apache License 2.0 or 0BSD: **Apache-2.0 OR 0BSD**. The licence choice is not a transfer of copyright ownership.

The current identified scope is the owner's rights in the exact code snapshots listed by path and SHA-256 in [licensing/owner-code.json](licensing/owner-code.json). [OWNERSHIP.md](OWNERSHIP.md) records the declaration supporting that scope and the unresolved questions. A listing is not a guarantee that every byte is copyrightable or exclusively owned; it does not grant rights belonging to anyone else. Material with no copyright is not made subject to copyright by this policy.

This additional grant does not cover third-party dependencies, another contributor's work, artwork, project names or trademarks. Adding a file to the project, editing an owner-listed file, or contributing through AI tools does not automatically make it eligible for 0BSD.

## Contributions and mixed ownership

Contributors retain copyright ownership of their own work. Unless a contributor explicitly agrees to different terms for a clearly identified contribution, their intentionally submitted contributions are licensed under Apache-2.0 only. No copyright assignment or agreement to 0BSD is required to contribute. Record any additional permission with the contributor's explicit assent and exact scope; a maintainer cannot supply that assent for them.

A file can contain owner portions available under Apache-2.0 OR 0BSD alongside another contributor's Apache-2.0-only portions or third-party material with its original terms. In that case describe each portion accurately. Do not mark the entire file 0BSD or Apache-2.0 OR 0BSD. Recipients must comply with all applicable terms when using the combined file; the owner's optional permission does not remove obligations on other portions.

The owner snapshot inventory is deliberately fixed by content hash, not a wildcard covering future edits. An unchanged snapshot remains covered by the recorded owner grant. For a modified file, establish which parts came from that snapshot and record the terms for new contributions before claiming any additional 0BSD scope. Do not update hashes in this inventory merely because a file changed: verify ownership and consent first. If provenance cannot be established, flag it for review and make no additional 0BSD claim for the uncertain portion.
